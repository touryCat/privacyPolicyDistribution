Privacy Policy

Mosaic Journey · Effective date: 2026-09-10

This Privacy Policy describes how Mosaic Journey (“the Game”), provided by Ploof Games Limited, handles your information.

Information We Collect

The Game does not automatically transmit personal information or gameplay records to Ploof Games Limited. It stores the local records described below. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

How We Use Information

Local records support progress, preferences and playtesting. If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond and investigate your request. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

Game Data and Features

Mosaic Journey is a single-player puzzle game. Its gameplay records remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Gameplay data: The Game stores progress, completed postcards, preferences, daily and weekly challenge records, coaching state, and gameplay counters such as sessions, moves, assists and completion times locally on your device. These records are not automatically sent to the developer.

Multiplayer and user content: The Game has no player accounts, multiplayer, chat or public user content.

Advertising and analytics: The Game has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK integrations. Local gameplay counters are used for playtesting and do not identify you.

Game services: The Game does not integrate Game Center, online leaderboards or developer-operated cloud synchronization.

In-app purchases: The Game has no in-app purchases.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local records remain in the app storage until that storage is removed. Restarting a stop does not erase all saved progress. Device backups may retain copies according to your device and backup settings. We cannot remotely delete records held only on your device.

Security

Protect access to your device and its backups. Local game records are not an encrypted vault. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The Game does not request personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact us with privacy questions or concerns about information sent to support.

Tracking

The Game does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Your Rights

There is no game account to delete. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information you sent to support. Contact us to make a request. For records held only on your device, manage the app storage and device backups.

Support, Diagnostics and This Website

If you choose to send an email or a playtest report, you decide what to share. The Game includes a local playtest report function that copies statistics to the clipboard when invoked; it does not send the report. Local diagnostic logs may include gameplay events.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion. Apple may process distribution, optional diagnostics or TestFlight feedback under its own policies and your settings.

The published policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening it connects your browser to GitHub, which may process connection information such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement. This page includes no advertising, analytics scripts or external fonts. Reading the bundled policy inside the Game does not require a website connection.

Device Access

The Game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or advertising tracking access.

Policy Changes

We may update this policy when the Game or our practices change. The effective date identifies the current text.
